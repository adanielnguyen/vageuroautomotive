FAQ

Common questions

Do you service the Volkswagen Amarok and Transporter?

Yes. We service and repair Amarok, Transporter, Caddy and Multivan, as well as VW passenger cars.

How often does my VW need a DSG service?

It depends on the gearbox. Wet-clutch DSGs generally need an oil and filter change at set intervals, while the 7-speed dry-clutch DQ200 doesn't have a regular gearbox oil change. Check your logbook or ask us.

Will a VW service with you affect my warranty?

No. We service to the logbook schedule with parts and oils that meet the specifications, so your warranty stays intact.
