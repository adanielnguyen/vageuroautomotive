Cooling system

VW water pump replacement

Coolant leaks and low coolant warnings are common on Volkswagen and Audi engines. We find the leak and replace the water pump, thermostat housing or hoses with quality parts.

Book a serviceCall (03) 8795 7699

On many Volkswagen and Audi petrol engines, the water pump and thermostat sit in a plastic housing that can crack or leak with age and heat. On diesels, the water pump is driven by the timing belt and is replaced with it.

Signs your water pump is failing

  • A low coolant warning, or topping up coolant often
  • Pink or purple crusty residue around the engine or under the car
  • A sweet smell after driving
  • The temperature gauge rising, or the engine overheating
  • Coolant drips under the front of the car

Engines we see it on

The 1.8 and 2.0 TSI (EA888) in the Golf, Golf GTI, Golf R, Tiguan, Passat, Skoda Octavia and Audi A3, A4 and Q5 are the most common. On 2.0 TDI engines, including the Amarok, Golf and Tiguan diesels, we replace the water pump as part of the timing belt service.

How we fix it

We pressure test the cooling system to find the exact leak, then replace the water pump, thermostat housing or hoses. We refill with coolant that meets VW's specification and bleed the system, so there are no air pockets.

Don't ignore a coolant leak

Running low on coolant can overheat the engine and damage the head gasket, which costs far more than a water pump. If your low coolant light is on, book a check or call (03) 8795 7699.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I drive with a leaking water pump?

Only for a short time while keeping the coolant topped up. A leak can get worse quickly and overheat the engine, so book it in as soon as you can.

Should the thermostat be replaced with the water pump?

On many TSI engines the water pump and thermostat share one housing, so they are often replaced together. We will tell you what your engine needs.
