VAG Euro Automotive is an independent Audi mechanic in Seaford. Audi shares its engines, gearboxes and electronics with the rest of the Volkswagen Group, and that family of cars is what we work on every day.

Audi logbook servicing

We service your Audi to the manufacturer's logbook schedule, use oils and parts that meet Audi's specifications, and stamp your service book. Servicing with an independent mechanic doesn't void your new-car warranty, so you can keep your Audi under warranty and still deal directly with the people working on it. Read more about logbook servicing and warranty.

Audi repairs we do

  • S tronic and DSG gearboxes: gearbox oil services and clutch faults
  • Mechatronic unit repair: diagnosis and repair of S tronic and DSG mechatronic units, the gearbox's control unit
  • Timing belts and chains: TFSI timing chains and tensioners, TDI timing belt kits and water pumps
  • quattro and Haldex: Haldex coupling services, differential oil services and all-wheel-drive faults
  • TDI diesels and DPF: diesel particulate filter (DPF) repairs, EGR faults and timing belts
  • Brakes: pads, rotors, fluid and electronic park brakes
  • Diagnostics: warning lights, fault codes, battery replacement and coding
  • Suspension and steering: control arms, bushes, shocks and wheel bearings
  • Air conditioning: re-gas, leak tests and repairs

Audi models we service

A1, A3, S3, RS 3, A4, S4, A5, S5, A6, Q2, Q3, Q5, SQ5, Q7, Q8 and TT, from older models through to cars still under new-car warranty.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you service my Audi while it is under warranty?

Yes. Using an independent mechanic doesn't void your Audi's new-car warranty as long as the servicing follows the manufacturer's specifications. We follow the logbook schedule and stamp your book.

Do you service Audi RS and S models?

Yes. We service and repair S and RS models such as the S3, RS 3, S4 and SQ5, as well as the standard range.

Is your workshop close to Frankston?

Yes. We are at 11 Stephenson Road in Seaford, a short drive from Frankston, Carrum Downs and Chelsea.
