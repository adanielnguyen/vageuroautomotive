DSG mechatronic unit repair in Seaford

Diagnosis and repair of DSG and S tronic mechatronic units for Volkswagen, Audi, Skoda and Cupra. Often the fix that saves replacing the whole gearbox.

The mechatronic unit is the brain of a DSG or S tronic gearbox. It combines the transmission's control electronics with the hydraulics that operate the clutches and shift the gears. When it fails, the gearbox can't shift properly.

Signs of a mechatronic fault

  • The gear indicator flashes, or a gearbox warning appears
  • The car drops into limp mode or loses some gears
  • Harsh, jerky or delayed shifts
  • The car won't select drive or reverse
  • Fault codes for gearbox pressure, solenoids or the control unit

How we repair it

We start by reading the fault codes and checking live data to confirm the mechatronic unit is the cause, rather than clutch wear or low oil. Depending on the fault, we repair or recondition the mechatronic unit, or quote for a replacement if it can't be repaired. Afterwards we run the basic settings and clutch adaptation and road test the car.

Gearboxes we see most

The 7-speed dry-clutch DQ200 (used with many 1.2 and 1.4 TSI engines) is the one most often affected. We also work on the 6-speed DQ250 and 7-speed DQ381 and DQ500 wet-clutch DSGs and Audi S tronic.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a new gearbox if the mechatronic unit fails?

Usually not. The mechatronic unit can often be repaired or replaced on its own, which costs far less than a new transmission.

Which cars have mechatronic problems?

Mostly Volkswagen, Audi, Skoda and Cupra with DSG or S tronic gearboxes, especially the 7-speed DQ200 fitted to many smaller TSI engines.
