DSG & transmission repair in Seaford

Gearbox servicing, diagnosis and repair for Volkswagen, Audi, Skoda and Cupra DSG and S tronic transmissions, from mechanics who work on them every week.

A dual-clutch DSG or S tronic gearbox needs a mechanic who knows it. We service, diagnose and repair Volkswagen Group transmissions at our Seaford workshop, a short drive from Frankston and Patterson Lakes.

Transmission problems we fix

  • Jerky, shuddering or slipping take-off from a standstill
  • Harsh, delayed or missed gear changes
  • A flashing gear indicator or a gearbox warning on the dash
  • Limp mode, or the car refusing to select drive or reverse
  • Noise or vibration from the gearbox

DSG and S tronic servicing

Wet-clutch DSG gearboxes need an oil and filter change at set intervals, often around every 60,000 km. We drain and refill with oil that meets VW's specification, replace the filter, check for fault codes and run the clutch adaptation where needed. Read our DSG service guide.

Mechatronic unit repair

Many DSG faults come from the mechatronic unit, the gearbox's built-in control unit. We diagnose it properly and repair the mechatronic unit where possible, instead of replacing the whole gearbox. More about mechatronic repair.

Automatic transmission servicing

We also service conventional automatic transmissions, such as those in the Touareg, Amarok and larger Audis, including fluid and filter changes and fault diagnosis.

Gearboxes we work on

6-speed DQ250 and 7-speed DQ200, DQ381 and DQ500 DSGs, Audi S tronic, and the automatic transmissions used across Volkswagen, Audi, Skoda and Cupra.

Common questions

How much does a DSG service cost?

It depends on the gearbox and the car. Call us on (03) 8795 7699 or book online with your model and we will quote before any work starts.

Can a faulty DSG be repaired instead of replaced?

Often, yes. Many faults come from the mechatronic unit or clutch adaptation, which can be repaired or reset without replacing the gearbox.
