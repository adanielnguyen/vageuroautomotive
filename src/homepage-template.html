Our story

Audi, Volkswagen and European vehicle service specialist.

VAG Euro Automotive has been looking after Volkswagen Group cars in Melbourne's south-east for more than five years. Workshop manager Vic and a team of qualified technicians service and repair everything from a daily Golf to an RS 3. We also look after Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, BMW and other European cars.
